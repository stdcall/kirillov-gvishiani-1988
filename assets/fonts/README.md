Cover assets
============

Oswald.ttf and Oswald-OFL.txt: Google Fonts, ofl/oswald; narrow Cyrillic
sans chosen to approximate the original binding's title lettering.
PTSans-Regular.ttf and PTSans-OFL.txt: Google Fonts, ofl/ptsans; authors.
Fonts are bundled, not installed in the operating system.

nauka-original.svg: https://naukapublishers.ru/assets/img/promo/logo-text.svg
nauka-emblem.svg: extracted emblem from the last compound path, cropped
to its own viewBox, coloured gold. The panel subpath was removed to turn
the modern negative emblem into the positive sun/book emblem of the
1988 binding. The book, sun, rays and rounded border retain the source
vector contours. The wordmark НАУКА and date 1727 are excluded.
The photo shows the same emblem structure; small printing wear and
photographic blur are not reproduced.

The cover uses vector artwork and bundled fonts.
